      Rules and tie-breaking

      Conventions used
      • Times are whole units. CT = completion time, TAT = CT − AT, WT = TAT − BT, RT = first CPU start − AT.
      • Priority: you choose whether the smaller or the larger number is higher priority (default: smaller number).
      • CPU idle time is drawn as a grey "Idle" block when no process has arrived yet.
      Tie-breaking (applied in this order)
      • FCFS: earliest arrival, then lower process number.
      • SJF: shortest burst, then earliest arrival, then lower process number.
      • SRTF and preemptive Priority: checked every time unit. The best value wins; on a tie the process already running keeps the CPU (no needless preemption), then earliest arrival, then lower process number.
      • Priority (non-preemptive): best priority, then earliest arrival, then lower process number.
      • Round Robin: ready queue is FIFO. A process arriving at the same moment a quantum ends is queued before the preempted process goes to the back.
